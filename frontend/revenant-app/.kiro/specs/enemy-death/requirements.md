# Requirements Document

## Introduction

The Enemy Death feature is responsible for handling the lifecycle of an enemy after it has been defeated.

When the Combat System determines that an enemy's health has reached zero, it emits an `EnemyDefeatedEvent`. The Enemy Death feature receives this event, stops every gameplay behavior associated with the enemy, plays the death animation, removes the enemy from the game world, and publishes an `EnemyRemovedEvent`.

The Enemy Death feature is responsible only for enemy removal from the game world.

The following functionality is explicitly out of scope:

- Damage calculation
- Combat resolution
- Experience rewards
- Gold rewards
- Loot generation
- Backend communication
- Respawn logic

---

## Glossary

| Term | Description |
|------|-------------|
| EnemyDefeatedEvent | Event generated by the Combat System when an enemy's health reaches zero. |
| Death Animation | Animation played after an enemy has been defeated. |
| EnemyRemovedEvent | Event indicating that an enemy has been completely removed from the game world. |
| Enemy Lifecycle | Sequence from enemy defeat until removal from the scene. |

---

## Requirements

## Requirement 1 - Process Enemy Defeat

**User Story:** As a developer, I want the Enemy Death feature to respond whenever an enemy is defeated.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an `EnemyDefeatedEvent` is received THEN the system SHALL begin the enemy death sequence.
2. EACH defeated enemy SHALL be processed exactly once.
3. IF an enemy has already entered the death sequence THEN additional defeat events SHALL be ignored.
4. THE death sequence SHALL begin immediately after receiving the event.

---

## Requirement 2 - Disable Enemy Behavior

**User Story:** As a player, I want defeated enemies to stop interacting with the game world.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the death sequence begins THEN the enemy SHALL stop moving.
2. WHEN the death sequence begins THEN the enemy AI SHALL be disabled.
3. WHEN the death sequence begins THEN the enemy SHALL no longer detect or pursue the player.
4. WHEN the death sequence begins THEN the enemy SHALL no longer be attackable.

---

## Requirement 3 - Play Death Animation

**User Story:** As a player, I want defeated enemies to play a death animation before disappearing.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the death sequence begins THEN the death animation SHALL start.
2. THE death animation SHALL play exactly once.
3. THE enemy SHALL remain visible while the animation is playing.
4. THE enemy SHALL not perform any other animation during the death sequence.

---

## Requirement 4 - Remove Enemy

**User Story:** As a player, I want defeated enemies to disappear from the world after the death animation finishes.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the death animation finishes THEN the enemy SHALL be removed from the active scene.
2. WHEN the enemy is removed THEN all associated resources SHALL be released.
3. THE removed enemy SHALL no longer participate in collisions.
4. THE removed enemy SHALL no longer be updated by the game loop.

---

## Requirement 5 - Publish Enemy Removal Event

**User Story:** As a developer, I want other gameplay systems to react when an enemy has been removed.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an enemy has been removed THEN the system SHALL publish an `EnemyRemovedEvent`.
2. THE event SHALL contain the removed enemy information.
3. EACH removed enemy SHALL generate exactly one `EnemyRemovedEvent`.
4. THE event SHALL not perform backend communication.

---

## Requirement 6 - Maintain Gameplay Consistency

**User Story:** As a player, I want defeated enemies to no longer affect gameplay.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. A defeated enemy SHALL not receive additional damage.
2. A defeated enemy SHALL not attack the player.
3. A defeated enemy SHALL not trigger collision events.
4. A defeated enemy SHALL not be considered by enemy AI systems.

---

## Requirement 7 - Local Execution

**User Story:** As a developer, I want enemy removal to execute locally without depending on the backend.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. The Enemy Death feature SHALL execute entirely on the client.
2. The Enemy Death feature SHALL not perform HTTP requests.
3. The Enemy Death feature SHALL not depend on backend availability.
4. Backend persistence SHALL be delegated to the Reward System after the enemy has been removed.

---

## Requirement 8 - Responsibility Separation

**User Story:** As a developer, I want the Enemy Death feature to remain focused only on the enemy removal lifecycle.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. The Enemy Death feature SHALL process `EnemyDefeatedEvent` objects.
2. The Enemy Death feature SHALL disable enemy behavior.
3. The Enemy Death feature SHALL play the death animation.
4. The Enemy Death feature SHALL remove enemies from the scene.
5. The Enemy Death feature SHALL publish `EnemyRemovedEvent`.
6. The Enemy Death feature SHALL not calculate damage.
7. The Enemy Death feature SHALL not calculate experience.
8. The Enemy Death feature SHALL not calculate gold rewards.
9. The Enemy Death feature SHALL not communicate with the backend.
10. The Enemy Death feature SHALL not update the player inventory.
11. The Enemy Death feature SHALL not persist game progress.
