# Requirements Document

## Introduction

The Combat System is responsible for resolving every attack performed during gameplay.

The system receives an `AttackRequest` generated by the Player Attack feature, calculates the damage dealt to each target, applies armor reduction, updates enemy health, determines whether enemies have been defeated, and emits the corresponding combat events.

All combat calculations are performed locally inside the game client.

The Combat System does not communicate with the backend. Backend communication is handled by the Reward System after an enemy has been defeated.

The following functionality is explicitly out of scope:

- Player attack detection
- Attack animations
- Enemy AI
- Reward persistence
- Inventory updates
- Experience calculation
- Gold calculation
- Loot generation

---

## Glossary

| Term | Description |
|------|-------------|
| AttackRequest | Request generated by the Player Attack feature containing the attacker and detected targets. |
| Combat Resolution | Process of calculating and applying combat results. |
| Damage | Amount of health removed from a target after armor reduction. |
| Armor | Defensive value used to reduce incoming damage. |
| Target | Enemy affected by an attack. |
| Combat Event | Event emitted after combat resolution for downstream systems. |

## Requirements

## Requirement 1 - Process Attack Requests

**User Story:** As a developer, I want the Combat System to process every attack generated by the Player Attack feature.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an `AttackRequest` is received THEN the Combat System SHALL begin combat resolution.
2. EACH `AttackRequest` SHALL be processed exactly once.
3. IF the request contains multiple targets THEN every target SHALL be processed independently.
4. IF the request contains no targets THEN the request SHALL complete successfully.

---

## Requirement 2 - Calculate Combat Damage

**User Story:** As a player, I want attacks to deal damage according to my attributes and the enemy's armor.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN combat is resolved THEN the system SHALL calculate damage for every target.
2. THE damage calculation SHALL consider the player's attack value.
3. THE damage calculation SHALL consider the target's armor value.
4. THE resulting damage SHALL never be negative.
5. THE final damage SHALL be applied independently to every target.

---

## Requirement 3 - Update Enemy Health

**User Story:** As a player, I want enemies to lose health after being hit.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN damage is calculated THEN the target's health SHALL be reduced.
2. THE target's health SHALL never become less than zero.
3. EVERY affected enemy SHALL update its own health independently.
4. THE health update SHALL occur immediately after damage calculation.

---

## Requirement 4 - Detect Enemy Death

**User Story:** As a player, I want enemies to be marked as defeated when they lose all their health.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an enemy's health reaches zero THEN the system SHALL mark the enemy as defeated.
2. A defeated enemy SHALL only be processed once.
3. Defeated enemies SHALL not receive additional damage.
4. Alive enemies SHALL remain available for future attacks.

---

## Requirement 5 - Emit Combat Events

**User Story:** As a developer, I want other gameplay systems to react to combat results.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. AFTER resolving an attack THEN the system SHALL emit a combat resolved event.
2. WHEN an enemy is defeated THEN the system SHALL emit an enemy defeated event.
3. Combat events SHALL contain the affected enemy information.
4. Combat events SHALL not perform backend communication.

---

## Requirement 6 - Support Multiple Targets

**User Story:** As a player, I want a single attack to affect multiple enemies inside the attack area.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN multiple enemies are included in an `AttackRequest` THEN every enemy SHALL be processed.
2. Damage calculation SHALL be independent for each enemy.
3. One enemy dying SHALL not interrupt combat resolution for the remaining enemies.
4. Combat SHALL finish after every target has been processed.

---

## Requirement 7 - Local Combat Resolution

**User Story:** As a developer, I want combat to execute locally without depending on network communication.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. Combat resolution SHALL occur entirely on the client.
2. Combat resolution SHALL not perform HTTP requests.
3. Combat resolution SHALL not depend on backend availability.
4. Reward persistence SHALL be delegated to another module after enemy defeat.

---

## Requirement 8 - Responsibility Separation

**User Story:** As a developer, I want the Combat System to remain focused on combat resolution only.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. The Combat System SHALL process `AttackRequest` objects.
2. The Combat System SHALL calculate damage.
3. The Combat System SHALL update enemy health.
4. The Combat System SHALL detect enemy death.
5. The Combat System SHALL emit combat events.
6. The Combat System SHALL not detect player input.
7. The Combat System SHALL not play attack animations.
8. The Combat System SHALL not spawn enemies.
9. The Combat System SHALL not calculate experience.
10. The Combat System SHALL not calculate gold rewards.
11. The Combat System SHALL not communicate with the backend.
12. The Combat System SHALL not persist game progress.
