# Combat System - Design Document

## Overview

The Combat System is responsible for resolving every attack executed during gameplay.

The system receives an `AttackRequest` generated by the Player Attack feature, processes every target independently, calculates the final damage, applies armor reduction, updates enemy health, determines whether an enemy has been defeated, and emits the corresponding combat events.

Combat resolution is performed entirely on the client and does not require backend communication.

The Reward System is responsible for persisting combat results after an enemy has been defeated.

The following functionality is explicitly out of scope:

- Player input
- Attack animations
- Enemy AI
- Reward calculation
- Experience calculation
- Gold calculation
- Loot generation
- Backend persistence

This specification complies with the architecture defined under `docs/architecture/`.

---

## Architecture

The Combat System acts as the central combat engine of the game.

It receives attack requests from the Player Attack module and publishes combat events for downstream systems.

## Components and Interfaces

## CombatSystem

### Responsibility

Coordinates combat resolution.

### Responsibilities

- Receive AttackRequest objects.
- Resolve every target independently.
- Calculate damage.
- Update enemy health.
- Detect defeated enemies.
- Publish combat events.

### Dependencies

- DamageCalculator
- Enemy
- Event Bus

## DamageCalculator

### Responsibility

Calculates the final damage dealt to a target.

### Responsibilities

- Read attacker attack value.
- Read target armor value.
- Calculate final damage.
- Guarantee non-negative damage.

### Input

```text
Attacker
Target
```

### Output

```text
Damage
```

### Responsibilities

- Store armor.

## Correctness Properties

## Combat Resolution

- Every AttackRequest SHALL be processed exactly once.
- Every target SHALL be processed independently.
- Combat SHALL continue even if one target is defeated.
- Combat SHALL complete after every target has been resolved.

---

## Damage Calculation

- Damage SHALL consider the attacker's attack value.
- Damage SHALL consider the target's armor value.
- Final damage SHALL never be negative.
- Damage calculation SHALL be deterministic for the same input values.

---

## Enemy Health

- Enemy health SHALL never become negative.
- Enemy health SHALL be updated immediately after damage calculation.
- Defeated enemies SHALL not receive additional damage.
- Every enemy SHALL maintain its own health state independently.

---

## Event Generation

- Every processed target SHALL generate one CombatResolvedEvent.
- Every defeated enemy SHALL generate one EnemyDefeatedEvent.
- Combat events SHALL never perform backend communication.

---

## Local Combat

- Combat SHALL execute entirely on the client.
- Combat SHALL not depend on network availability.
- Combat SHALL never perform HTTP requests.
- Backend persistence SHALL be delegated to the Reward System after enemy defeat.

---

## Separation of Responsibilities

- CombatSystem SHALL only resolve combat.
- CombatSystem SHALL not detect player input.
- CombatSystem SHALL not play animations.
- CombatSystem SHALL not calculate rewards.
- CombatSystem SHALL not update the HUD.
- CombatSystem SHALL not persist player progression.
- CombatSystem SHALL not communicate with the backend.

---

# Error Handling

## Empty AttackRequest

If an AttackRequest contains no targets:

- Combat resolution completes successfully.
- No damage is calculated.
- No events are emitted.

---

## Invalid Target

If a target is invalid or unavailable:

- The target is skipped.
- The error is logged.
- Remaining targets continue processing.

---

## Already Defeated Enemy

If a target has already been defeated:

- Damage calculation is skipped.
- No duplicate defeat event is emitted.
- Combat continues normally.

---

## Damage Calculation Failure

If damage cannot be calculated:

- The affected target is skipped.
- The error is logged.
- Remaining targets continue processing.

# Testing Strategy

## Unit Testing

Verify:

- Damage calculation.
- Armor reduction.
- Enemy health updates.
- Enemy death detection.
- CombatResult generation.
- Combat event generation.

---

## Integration Testing

Verify:

- AttackRequest is received from the Event Bus.
- Every target is processed.
- Enemy health is updated correctly.
- CombatResolvedEvent is published.
- EnemyDefeatedEvent is published only once.
- No backend communication occurs during combat.

---

## End-to-End Testing

Verify the following gameplay flow:

```text
Player performs an attack

↓

AttackRequest is generated

↓

CombatSystem receives the request

↓

Damage is calculated

↓

Enemy health is updated

↓

Enemy defeated?

      │
  No  │  Yes
      │
      ▼

CombatResolvedEvent

EnemyDefeatedEvent

↓

RewardSystem receives the defeat event

↓

Reward persistence is handled separately
```

The feature is considered complete when every `AttackRequest` is resolved locally, enemy health is updated correctly, defeated enemies generate a single defeat event, and no backend communication occurs during combat resolution.
